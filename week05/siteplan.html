Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a central location for managing supplier information for a company that sells parts for agricultural and construction equipment. Users will be able to view supplier details, browse product categories, access purchasing policies, and learn about the purchasing team.

Scenarios

  • How can I find contact information for a specific supplier?
  • Where can I view the purchasing policies and supplier requirements?

Color Scheme

  • Dark Green (#2E5E3E): Used for headings, navigation, and buttons.
  • Light Gray (#F4F4F4): Used for the page background and content sections.

Typography

  • Roboto: Used for headings and navigation menus.
  • Open Sans: Used for paragraphs and general content.

AgriParts Hub

AgriParts Hub is a supplier management website designed for a company that sells parts for agricultural and construction equipment. The site helps organize supplier information, product categories, purchasing policies, and contact details in one central location. The name was chosen because it represents a central hub for supplier and purchasing management.
